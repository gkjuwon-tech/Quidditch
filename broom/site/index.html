Three impossible things, made possible

It works because of three rules.

Nobody falls

Low flight, a geofenced pitch, a chute, and a kill switch that lands you softly instead of cutting the motors. The vehicle's enemy is gravity. We planned for gravity.

Nobody gets hit

Every ball is a padded shell over a micro-drone. A stopping-distance floor means it decelerates to a halt at its safety distance from any person — proven across full matches.

Nothing collides

A central referee deconflicts brooms diving for the same Snitch. Three independent avoidance layers, one unbreakable rule.
